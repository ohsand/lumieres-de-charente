Gîte Rose is the heart of the domaine for larger groups. The beamed living room opens onto a dining room with room for a long family table, and the patio at the back catches the evening sun.

After the winter renovation Rose has four bedrooms: two doubles and two twins, so it sleeps eight comfortably. Two shower rooms keep the mornings calm.

Old French furniture, linen curtains and a few brocante treasures give each room its own character.
