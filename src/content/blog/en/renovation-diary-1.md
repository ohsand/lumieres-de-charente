Every renovation starts with a walk around with a notebook. This week we measured every room in the main house, marked which old beams can stay and argued (nicely) about colours.

Our rule: keep everything with character, and make everything else warm and comfortable. Old terracotta tiles are being numbered and lifted so they can be relaid. Doors from a local brocante are waiting in the barn.

Next time: the first walls come down.
