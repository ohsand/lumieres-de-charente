After two seasons of welcoming guests, we felt the domaine deserved a name that matched its atmosphere. *Lumières de Charente* – the lights of the Charente – is about the soft evening light over the fields, but also about the warm glow of the gîtes in winter.

This winter we start the biggest renovation since the farm was converted: a fifth gîte in the main house, a new Gîte Olive, an extra bedroom in Rose and a second pool.

We will share the progress here, with photos and short videos. Follow along!
