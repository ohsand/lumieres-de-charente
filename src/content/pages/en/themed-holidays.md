Want more than a pool and a deckchair? Choose one of our themes and we will prepare routes, addresses and reservations for you.

## Brocante & flea markets
A week of hunting for French antiques: we give you a calendar of the best markets and brocantes nearby, and tips on shipping larger finds home.

## Cognac & Pineau
Visits to family distilleries and cognac houses, a tasting of Pineau des Charentes and lunch among the vineyards.

## Cycling & walking
Mapped routes from the domaine along quiet lanes, sunflower fields and riverside villages. Bike hire can be arranged.

## Family gatherings
Book the whole domaine – five gîtes, 27 beds – for a reunion, anniversary or *cousinade*. Long tables under the trees included.

## Winter by the fire
From November to March: warm gîtes, soup from the market, walks in the frost and evenings with a glass of cognac. Short breaks welcome.
