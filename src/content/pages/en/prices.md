## Included in the price
- Bed linen, bath towels and a final clean
- Wi-Fi, heating and electricity
- Use of the pools (late May – end of September), garden, games room and barbecues
- Cots, high chairs and extra kitchen equipment on request

## Good to know
- **Arrival** from 16:00, **departure** by 10:00. Saturday to Saturday in July and August; flexible the rest of the year.
- **Short breaks** (minimum 3 nights) are possible outside July and August – ask us for a price.
- **Booking**: a 30% deposit confirms your stay; the balance is due six weeks before arrival.
- **Security deposit**: €300 per gîte, refunded after your stay.
- **Tourist tax** is charged by the local council per adult per night.
- **The whole domaine** (27 guests) can be booked for family celebrations and *cousinades*. House rules apply.
- Payment in euros by bank transfer. No booking or service fees when you book directly with us.
