Lumières de Charente is a small domaine of five gîtes in Brettes, in the green north of the Charente. We renovated the old farm with flea-market finds, linen and lime-washed walls, so it feels like a French family house rather than a holiday park. Each gîte is self-contained with its own kitchen and terrace – book one for your family, or several for a gathering of friends.
