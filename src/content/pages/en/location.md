The domaine lies on the edge of Brettes, a small village of stone houses about 11 km from Ruffec, in the north of the Charente department (Nouvelle-Aquitaine). It is one of the sunniest parts of western France.

**Address:** 1 Impasse des Tilleuls, 16240 Brettes

## Getting here
| | Approx. distance |
|---|---|
| Ruffec (shops, station) | 15 min |
| Angoulême (TGV station) | 50 min |
| Poitiers (TGV station, airport) | 1 h |
| Cognac | 1 h |
| La Rochelle and the Atlantic coast | 1 h 45 |
| Bordeaux (airport) | 2 h 15 |

**By car:** the A10 motorway (Paris–Bordeaux) and the N10 are close by. From the Channel ports of Caen, Le Havre or St-Malo allow around five to six hours.

**By train:** high-speed TGV trains from Paris reach Angoulême in under two hours; some also stop at Ruffec. We can recommend a taxi or help with car hire.

**By air:** the nearest airports are Poitiers, Limoges, La Rochelle and Bordeaux.
