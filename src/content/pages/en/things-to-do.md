## Outdoors
- Canoeing and swimming in the River Charente and local lakes
- Cycling and walking straight from the gate – quiet lanes and marked footpaths
- The **Vélorail** near Confolens: pedal a rail-bike along an old railway line

## Markets and brocantes
Every weekend there are village markets, *vide-greniers* and brocante fairs nearby – perfect for finding a piece of old France to take home. We happily share our favourite addresses.

## Days out with children
- **Futuroscope** theme park near Poitiers (about an hour)
- **La Vallée des Singes**, a monkey park where the animals roam free
- **La Planète des Crocodiles** in Civaux
- A large water park near Angoulême

## Culture and taste
Visit a cognac house in Cognac or Jarnac, Romanesque churches across the Charente and the historic towns of Angoulême and Poitiers.

## At the domaine
Two pools, a big garden, table tennis and a games room – some guests hardly leave.
