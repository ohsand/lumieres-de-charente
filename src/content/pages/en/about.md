We are Mathieu and Yann. We both had careers in other fields and lived in big cities for many years. The Charente started as a holiday destination, then became the place we kept coming back to – and finally, home. Like many people we first dreamed of Provence, but found that the quieter, slower life here suited us much better.

We took over the gîtes from Mark and Sarah, who welcomed guests here for more than ten years. We live nearby with our children and are always around to help – in English, French, German or Dutch.

## What we are working on
During the winter of 2026/27 we are renovating the domaine: the main house becomes a fifth gîte, Gîte Olive is being completely renewed and Gîte Rose gains an extra bedroom. A second pool, and perhaps a sauna and hot tub, will follow. You can follow every step in our [diary](/en/blog/).

> We want guests to feel they are staying with friends in a French house – not in a holiday park.
