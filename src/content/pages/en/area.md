The north Charente is rural France as it used to be: rolling farmland, sunflower and wheat fields, quiet lanes and villages with a church, a bakery and a café. Life here follows the seasons and the markets.

## Close by
- **The next village** (2.5 km): small grocery shops and a bakery for your morning croissants.
- **Ruffec** and the surrounding towns: cafés, restaurants and large supermarkets about 15 minutes away.
- **Verteuil-sur-Charente, Tusson and Nanteuil-en-Vallée**: beautiful small villages for a stroll, a coffee or an ice cream by the river.

## A little further
- **Cognac and Jarnac** (about an hour): cognac houses, vineyards and riverside lunches.
- **Angoulême**: old ramparts, cathedral and the famous comic-strip festival.
- **Poitiers, La Rochelle** and the Atlantic beaches make easy day trips.

## Local flavours
Try *Pineau des Charentes*, a fortified wine served chilled as an apéritif, local goat's cheese, farm bread and, of course, Charentais melons in summer.
